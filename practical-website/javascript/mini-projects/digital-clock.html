Real-Time Digital Clock & Live Calendar

A precision real-time digital clock synchronized with system time, featuring dynamic greeting messages, 12-hour / 24-hour format toggling, and live date formatting.

📘 How It Works & Academic Notes

  • Date Object: Instantiates new Date() to extract local hours, minutes, seconds, day of week, and full year.
  • Timing Loop: Executes every 1000 milliseconds via setInterval(updateClock, 1000) to guarantee sub-second refresh fidelity.
  • Zero-Padding: Employs String.prototype.padStart(2, '0') so single-digit numbers (like 7 seconds) render gracefully as 07.
